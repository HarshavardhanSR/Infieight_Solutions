Frequently asked questions

What is included in a product design engagement?

It can include discovery, user journeys, wireframes, interface design, prototypes, design systems, and usability reviews. We agree on the right scope after learning about your product.

Can you work with our developers?

Yes. We can collaborate with your existing engineers, document design decisions, and support implementation reviews through launch.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the product, team, and scope. We break the work into clear milestones and confirm a realistic schedule before starting.
